CBSE Board Exam PYQ

Louisa as a Perfect Wife[cite: 1]

Question:

Louisa behaves like a perfect wife. Give evidence from 'The Third Level' in support of the above statement[cite: 1].

Answer Formulation[cite: 1]:

Louisa's concern for Charley reflects her as an ideal, supportive wife. When Charley becomes obsessed with finding the "third level,” she does not ridicule him; instead, she worries for his well-being and urges him to stop searching, fearing he might get hurt[cite: 1]. Later, when Sam's letter appears, Louisa joins Charley in looking for the third level—showing trust, understanding, and companionship[cite: 1]. Her patience and emotional support portray her as a perfect partner[cite: 1].

CBSE Board Exam PYQ

Charley's Fascination with Galesburg[cite: 1]

Question:

Why was Charley fascinated with Galesburg?[cite: 1]

Answer Formulation[cite: 1]:

Charley saw Galesburg as a peaceful, idyllic town untouched by the anxieties of the modern world[cite: 1]. Its long summer evenings, friendly people, old houses, and calm lifestyle represented security and warmth—qualities missing in Charley's stressed urban life[cite: 1]. He longed for a world before the two World Wars, where people enjoyed simple pleasures[cite: 1]. Galesburg symbolised innocence, stability, and emotional comfort[cite: 1].
